# Community conduct

Be respectful, precise and constructive. Critique code and evidence, not people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussions relevant and make room for disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, unwanted sexual attention, spam and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Never post credentials or private mailbox data in a community space.

The repository maintainer, [@pythc](https://github.com/pythc), may edit or remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when needed. Moderation should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact; repeated or severe abuse may lead to removal from project spaces.

Use GitHub's **Report content** controls for abuse or private safety concerns. Do not create public issues containing sensitive details. For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md). This volunteer project does not offer a confidential conduct-reporting service or guaranteed response time.
